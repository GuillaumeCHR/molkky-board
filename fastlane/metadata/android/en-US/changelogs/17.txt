First public release of Molkky Board!
- Tap the pins, the app keeps score with the official Mölkky rules
- Fix any past throw: everything is recalculated
- Several phones on the same game, no account, no internet
- Game curve, history, Hall of Fame and badges
- Share the moment as a picture or a short video
- No ads, no trackers
Since the test: the curve now shows when a player goes over 50 before dropping to 25.
