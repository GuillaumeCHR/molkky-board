Molkky Board: Mölkky Scorer

## Full description (4000 max) — 3377 characters

Molkky Board keeps score of your Mölkky games so everyone can keep their eyes on the pins. The app applies the rules, fixes mistakes without fuss and brings every throw to life.

Several phones, one game
• Scan the organizer's QR code and your phone joins the game in seconds.
• Any phone can enter the throws, and scores update live on every phone. A friend who isn't playing can join as referee and keep score for everyone.
• No account, no server, no internet access: phones talk directly to each other, encrypted, over the same Wi-Fi or a hotspot created by the organizer's phone. It works at the back of the garden, on the beach or at the campsite.
• The organizer approves every newcomer and can disconnect a phone at any time. Every phone is told when someone leaves or the game ends.

Fast, mistake-proof scoring
• Two ways to enter a throw: tap the fallen pins, or use the quick pad, with a clear choice between "single pin" and "number of pins".
• A preview before you confirm ("+7 → 38"): nothing is saved until you tap OK, so no turn is skipped by accident.
• Entered a throw wrong? Edit or delete any throw, even several rounds later: scores, drops back to 25, eliminations and the winner are all recalculated.
• Undo the last throw with a single tap.

Every rule, applied for you
• Exactly 50 points, back to 25 if you go over, out after three misses in a row.
• Solo or team play, with a throwing order you set by drag and drop and can still change between rounds.
• Someone arrives late? Add them mid-game. Someone has to leave? Remove them.
• Advanced rules: target score, what happens when you go over, what happens after three misses, and a family mode to balance skill levels.
• The round number is always on screen, and the full scoreboard is one tap away.
• Two-set matches with the official tie-break, the Mölkkout.

New to the game?
• A rules guide with simple animated diagrams and answers to the most common questions.
• The starting formation of the pins and the throwing distance, at a glance.
• A guided first game with a few tips, no video to watch.

Every throw becomes a moment
• Animations for victories, eliminations and the famous "12!".
• Optional offline voice announcements: "Léa to throw, needs 7".
• Named teams with their own color and emblem, building up their track record game after game.
• A Hall of Fame for players and teams: wins, fastest win, perfect game, and more records and badges.
• Statistics and a complete history of your games.
• Your best moments as an image or a short video, created on your phone and ready to share.

Reliable, even in bright sunlight
• The game is saved after every throw: if the app closes, you pick up right where you left off.
• Scores stay readable with large font sizes, a high-contrast "Bright sunlight" mode, and the screen stays on.
• Works fully offline. In English and French.

Your privacy, respected
• No advertising, no trackers, no audience measurement tools.
• No account and no data collected: your games stay on your phones.
• Only nicknames, emblems and throws are shared with the other phones of the game. Player photos never leave your phone.
• Free software under the GNU GPL v3: the source code is public.
